"Is something on my phone watching me" is a normal question with a hard
answer. Sweep checks the places surveillance software actually lives:

* Installed apps against a public, maintained stalkerware list (Echap's
  indicators, bundled, matched by package name and signing certificate so
  renamed copies still match).
* Device admin apps, which can lock, wipe, and resist removal.
* Enabled accessibility services, which can read the screen.
* Apps with no launcher icon, and apps installed from outside any store.

Every finding is explained in words. Sweep never says "you are safe": it
says "these specific checks found nothing", and means exactly that.
Matches lead with do-not-confront guidance and advocate hotlines, because
removing stalkerware can escalate a dangerous situation.

One permission (listing installed apps, which is the checkup itself) and
no internet permission, so what Sweep sees cannot leave your phone.
Results are never stored. A Leave fast button sits on every screen.

Runs on Android 9 and newer, with Android System WebView 87 or newer.
Android 9 has had no security fixes since January 2022, so on a phone that
old the checkup tells you less: spyware that got in through an unpatched
hole might not show up as an app.

Beta: offered to domain organizations for review. Treat Sweep as one
tool, not an authority.
