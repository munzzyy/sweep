Privacy

Sweep looks at sensitive things, so this page is blunt:

  • The checkup reads the list of installed apps, active device admins, and enabled accessibility services. That reading happens on your phone and goes nowhere: the app has no internet permission, so the operating system will not let it transmit anything, ever.
  • Results are never stored. Leave the results screen and they are gone; there is no history, no log, no file.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. One preference is kept: your language.
  • The stalkerware list ships inside the app (from Echap's public CC-BY dataset) and is never fetched at runtime.
  • The Leave fast button closes everything immediately and removes the app from your recent apps list.
